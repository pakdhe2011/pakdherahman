Unit 1: Professional Greetings & Hotel Facilities

Materi pembelajaran standar pelayanan penerimaan tamu dan deskripsi fasilitas hotel.

Key Expressions (Ungkapan Kunci)

Function Expressions (English) Terjemahan
Welcoming Guests "Good morning, welcome to Grand Heritage Hotel." Selamat pagi, selamat datang di Hotel Grand Heritage.
Offering Assistance "How may I assist you today, Sir/Madam?" Ada yang bisa saya bantu, Pak/Bu?

Roleplay Script: Front Desk Greeting

Receptionist: Good afternoon! Welcome to The Green Palm Resort. How may I help you today?

Guest: Good afternoon. I have a reservation under the name of Smith.

Receptionist: Certainly, Mr. Smith. May I have your ID card, please?
